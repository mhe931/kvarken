## Core defense claim

A small, typed, bounded, provenance-aware Earth Observation metadata infrastructure can provide a credible and reproducible research and education baseline for the Kvarken Space Center context.

## Evidence discipline

The 256-scene benchmark is a controlled synthetic metadata baseline. It is not presented as universal CDSE or raster-download performance. The dated 20260915 artifact is explicitly an offline-fallback run.
