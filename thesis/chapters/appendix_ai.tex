\chapter{Disclosure of AI-Assisted Work}

Generative AI tools were used in the preparation of this thesis for substantial drafting assistance, restructuring, language editing, quality review, and preparation of supporting presentation material. The final compilation used OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-5.6 Sol). The author reviewed, selected, revised, and accepted the final text and remains responsible for the research questions, system design, scientific claims, quantitative results, citations, limitations, and conclusions \citep{openai-chatgpt,uwasa-writing}.

AI-generated text was not treated as empirical evidence. Numerical results reported in the thesis were taken from the project evidence described in the methodology and results chapters. Where AI-assisted restructuring introduced explanatory synthesis, the wording was constrained to the implemented system and the evidence boundaries already documented by the project. No live-provider results were invented to replace missing measurements.
